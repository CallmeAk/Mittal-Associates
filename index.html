About the Firm

Boutique Legal Excellence. Strategic Commercial Thinking.

At Mittal & Associates, we bridge the gap between traditional legal rigor and modern commercial strategy. Headquartered in New Delhi, we are a boutique law firm with a focused Intellectual Property practice, brand-protection capability, and high-stakes Commercial Litigation experience.

We do not merely resolve disputes; we anticipate them. Whether securing trademark portfolios, navigating indirect taxation matters, or litigating before constitutional courts, the firm delivers precise, strategic, and uncompromising legal representation.

We protect your brand, your business, and your future.

Our Team

Ayush Mittal

Advocate, Supreme Court of India | Panel Counsel, Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD)

Ayush Mittal is a legal professional and the driving force behind Mittal & Associates. Practicing extensively before the Supreme Court of India and the Delhi High Court, his work focuses on Intellectual Property and Commercial Litigation.

As Panel Counsel for the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, his practice operates at the intersection of public administration law and commercial strategy. He advises individuals, commercial entities, and Resident Welfare Associations on trademark infringement, indirect taxation, and civil disputes.

His approach combines precise legal drafting with a proactive understanding of evolving jurisprudence.
