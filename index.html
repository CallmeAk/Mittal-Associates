About the Firm

Trusted Legal Counsel. Practical Solutions.

Mittal & Associates is a law practice dedicated to delivering strategic, comprehensive, and result-oriented legal solutions. The firm advises clients across commercial litigation, intellectual property rights, contractual matters, indirect taxation, and real estate disputes.

The firm combines legal precision with commercial understanding, ensuring every matter is approached with diligence, discretion, and a clear focus on protecting client rights and business interests.
